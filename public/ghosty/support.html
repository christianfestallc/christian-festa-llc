Common Questions (FAQ)

  • How do I turn Ghosty on (iPhone or iPad)?
    Open Ghosty and tap Enable Ghosty. iOS asks to add a VPN configuration — tap Allow and confirm with Face ID or your passcode. There is no VPN server: the configuration is how iOS lets Ghosty inspect your device's own connections. It appears in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management as “Ghosty”.
  • Free vs Premium?
    Free: all five curated categories, today's blocked lookups and Insights, and up to 10 domains on your own blocklist.
    Premium: every lookup (allowed as well as blocked), 7-day and 30-day Insights, real-time protection for tracker-looking domains that aren't on any list, an unlimited blocklist, and your choice of DNS resolver. On the Mac, Premium also shows which app contacted what, and lets you exempt one app. Monthly, yearly, or a family plan for up to six people.
    One subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Restore purchases from the Premium screen.
  • Nothing is blocking?
    Check that protection is on from Home, that Ghosty isn't paused, and that at least one category is switched on under Protection → Categories.
  • Can I turn it off for a while?
    Yes. Switch protection off from Home and choose 15 minutes, 1 hour, or until tomorrow morning. Ghosty turns itself back on at the deadline, and you can remove the configuration any time in iOS Settings.
  • The configuration won’t install?
    Ghosty needs iOS 18 or later. If you tapped Don't Allow, tap Enable Ghosty again. Restart your device if the prompt never appears.

On the Mac

  • How do I turn Ghosty on?
    Ghosty needs macOS 15 or later and has to run from your Applications folder; if it isn't there, it offers to move itself. Click Enable Ghosty, then: in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions, under Network Extensions, switch Ghosty on; when macOS asks to add proxy configurations, click Allow; when it asks to filter network content, click Allow. A ghost appears in the menu bar.
  • Does Ghosty need to stay open?
    Yes. Ghosty protects your Mac while it's running in the menu bar. Closing the window keeps it there. Choosing Quit Ghosty from the menu bar ghost turns protection off until you open it again. Turn on Settings › General › Launch at login so it starts with your Mac.
  • Can I pause it?
    Click the menu bar ghost and switch protection off, or press ⌃⌥⌘G from any app. Choose 15 minutes, 1 hour, or overnight, and Ghosty turns itself back on.
  • Which app is talking to whom?
    With Premium, Activity › By app lists every app and the domains it contacted. Switch off Ghosty checks for an app to let it through while the rest of your Mac stays protected.
  • Already subscribed on your iPhone?
    Open the Premium screen on the Mac and choose Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • How do I turn it off completely?
    Switch protection off in Ghosty, quit it, or switch Ghosty off in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.

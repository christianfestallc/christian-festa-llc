Ghosty

Privacy-first DNS filter

Common Questions

How do I turn on DNS filtering?
Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → DNS → Add Ghosty.
Free vs Premium?
Free: 3 custom domains + only sees blocked traffic.
Premium: Unlimited custom domains + full traffic log view.
Nothing is blocking?
Make sure the DNS proxy is enabled and you’ve toggled on at least one curated list.
Network extension won’t install?
iOS 18+ only. Restart your iPhone and try again.
Want to suggest a blocklist?
Email us — we add popular ones every week.

Walkout Intros

AI-powered game-day announcements

Common Questions

How do I generate a walk-up intro?
Tap the + button, enter player name/number, type or edit the script, choose an AI voice (Pro), pick a song from Apple Music or your library, then preview and save.
Free vs Pro?
Free: Limited teams & players. One AI voice option.
Pro: Unlimited teams, unlimited players, multiple AI voices. 1-week intro offer on both plans.
Restore purchases?
Go to Settings inside the app and tap “Restore Purchases”.
AI voice not generating?
Make sure you have a stable internet connection (AI generation requires it). Try a shorter script or different voice.
Can I use my own custom scripts?
Yes! You can fully edit the AI-generated script or write your own from scratch.
Lineup playback order?
Create a lineup for a specific game, then play the intros in order — perfect for the dugout, sideline, or announcer booth.
